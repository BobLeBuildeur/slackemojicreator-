# Product Vision

## Overview

Slack Emoji Creator helps people make Slack-ready emojis from familiar characters. Customers pick a character, a situation, and a target, then receive an image they can add to Slack.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants custom Slack emojis without designing them from scratch—teams, communities, and individuals who already share a shared visual language of characters and situations.

## What it does

The product turns a simple choice into a finished emoji:

1. **Choose a character** — Select from common characters people already recognize and use in chat culture.
2. **Choose a situation** — Apply a scenario or pose, such as a Pepe hat or a Bufo offering.
3. **Choose a target** — Specify what the emoji is about or directed toward.
4. **Get a Slack-ready image** — Receive an image prepared for use as a Slack emoji.

## Generators

The product includes multiple generators for individual characters and situations. Each generator focuses on a specific character, vibe, or scenario so the result feels intentional rather than generic.

Examples of the kinds of generators the product supports:

- Character-led looks (for example, Pepe)
- Situation-led moments (for example, Bufo offering)
- Combinations of character and situation that match how people actually react in Slack

New generators can be added over time as more characters and situations become useful to customers.

## Core experience

The customer experience stays simple:

- Browse or select a **character**
- Pick a **situation**
- Set a **target**
- Generate an image ready to add to Slack

The product’s job is to make that path fast, clear, and fun—from idea to emoji—without asking customers to design or assemble the image themselves.

## Guiding principles

- **Familiar characters first** — Lean on characters people already know.
- **Situations that travel** — Capture moments people actually use in conversation.
- **Slack-ready by default** — Output should be usable as a Slack emoji with no extra creative work.
- **Choice over complexity** — Character, situation, and target are enough to get a great result.
- **Expand through generators** — Grow the library of characters and situations rather than complicating the core flow.

## Success looks like

Customers can go from “I need an emoji for this moment” to a Slack-ready image by choosing a character, a situation, and a target—and feel that the result fits the culture of their workspace.
